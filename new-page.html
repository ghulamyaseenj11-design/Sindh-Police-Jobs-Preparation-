Typical Recruitment Stages

Apply

Read the official advertisement and submit your application on time.

Documents

Keep CNIC, domicile, certificates and photos ready.

Physical Test

Height/chest checks and running or fitness events.

Written Test

MCQs covering GK, science, English, Islamiat and math.

Interview

Personality, knowledge and confidence are checked.

GET FIT

Physical Test Training Plan

A beginner-friendly 4-week plan to build stamina and strength. Adjust it to your own fitness level.

💧 HydrationDrink enough water through the day, especially before and after running.
😴 SleepSleep 7–8 hours. Muscles recover and grow while you rest.
🍳 FoodEat eggs, milk, dal, roti and fruit. Avoid heavy oily food before training.
🦵 Warm-upAlways warm up for 5–10 minutes and stretch after running to avoid injury.
⚠️ Physical standards (height, chest, running time and so on) are set in each official advertisement, so check them there. If you have a health problem, ask a doctor before starting hard training.
