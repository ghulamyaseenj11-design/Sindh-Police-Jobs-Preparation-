About Study with GYJ

A free preparation platform for Sindh government job aspirants, made by a candidate like you.

Who We Are

Study with GYJ is a free exam preparation website made by Ghulam Yaseen, from Kashmore, Sindh. I made this website while preparing for the Jail Police (Prison Constable) exam myself, because good preparation material was hard to find in one place — and much of it was not free.

What We Do

We bring together, in one place and free of charge:

  • Mock tests — practice tests with instant results for Jail Police, Vaccinators, Islamabad Police and PST JST, covering English, Mathematics, Science, Computer, General Knowledge, Islamiat and more.
  • Study notes — short, simple notes you can read and download as PDF.
  • Model past papers — practice papers made in the pattern of past tests, so you can see how the real paper feels.
  • Department guides — simple preparation guides for each department and each stage of the process.
  • Blogs and updates — preparation tips and the latest updates about recruitment.

You can also create a free account to keep your recently viewed notes, reading history and saved blogs in one place.

Why Free

Preparation should not cost money. Every mock test, note and paper on this website is free, and will stay free. We do not charge for anything on this website.

What We Are Not

Study with GYJ is an independent educational website. It is not the official website of Sindh Police, Sindh Prisons and Corrections Service, PTS, or any government department. Our tests and notes are practice material only. For official dates, results, merit lists and notices, always check the official department and testing service websites.
