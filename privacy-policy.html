Privacy Policy

How Study with GYJ collects, uses and protects your information. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Study with GYJ is a free exam preparation website run by Ghulam Yaseen. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using this website, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Account information. If you create an account, we collect the name and email address you provide, and your password. Passwords are stored in encrypted form by our login provider (Supabase) — we never see or store your password in plain text. You may also add an optional profile photo, which is stored in our file storage.

Activity saved on your device. Your recently viewed study notes, reading history, saved blogs and notification settings are stored in your own browser (local storage) on your own device. This information is not sent to our servers.

What we do not collect. We do not ask for your CNIC number, address, bank details, payment information or location. Please never send us passwords, OTP codes or CNIC numbers by message.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and run your account, and to show your profile, saved blogs and recent activity.
  • To let you change your password and manage your notification settings.
  • To keep the website running and to fix problems.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with anyone for marketing.

3. Cookies and Advertising

This website uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in and to remember your activity on your device. It does not use tracking cookies for its own features.

We may show advertisements from Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this website and other websites. You can turn off personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com and at aboutads.info/choices. You can also block cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the website may then not work properly.

4. Third-Party Services

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Supabase for accounts and site data. These services process information only to run the website, under their own privacy policies. The website also links to official department and testing service websites; their privacy rules apply when you visit them.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, including encrypted passwords and accounts that only you can open with your own password. No website is completely secure, so please keep your password private and do not share it with anyone.

6. Children's Privacy

This website is made for job aspirants preparing for government tests. If you are under 18, please use it with the permission of a parent or guardian.

7. Your Choices

You can edit your name and photo and change your password from your profile page, and you can log out at any time. To close your account and have your account information removed, contact us at the email address below and we will delete it.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any question about this policy, contact us at ghulamyasinj11@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at 0333-7363359.
